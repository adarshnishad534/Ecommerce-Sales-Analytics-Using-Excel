# E-commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard | Excel

## 📊 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing E-commerce sales data using Microsoft Excel to understand sales performance, profitability, product categories, and regional business trends.

An interactive dashboard was developed using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and Slicers to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

- Pivot Tables

- Data Cleaning

## 📈 Dashboard Features

1. **Sales & Profit Analysis** – Overall sales and profit performance.
2. **Category-wise Profit Analysis** – Profit contribution from Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.
3. **Top 5 States by Sales** – Identifies the highest-performing states.
4. **Sub-Category-wise Sales Analysis** – Compares sales across product sub-categories.
5. **Category-wise Profit Comparison** – Evaluates profitability across product categories.
6. **Interactive Filters** – Region-wise and category-wise filtering using Slicers.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
- Evaluate profitability and profit margins.
- Identify top-performing states.
- Understand category-wise and sub-category-wise performance.
- Discover regional sales trends.
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
- Total sales reached ₹22.97 lakh.
- Total profit was ₹2.86 lakh.
- Overall profit margin was 12.47%.
- Technology contributed approximately 37% of category-wise profit.
- Furniture contributed approximately 32% of category-wise profit.
- Office Supplies contributed approximately 31% of category-wise profit.
- California recorded the highest sales among the displayed top five states.
